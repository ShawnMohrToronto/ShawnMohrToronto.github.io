LEAP
In development
B2B Lead Enrichment Agent Proxy — Solo founder build · Program management & technical execution
The problem

Revenue Operations teams at mid-market B2B firms share a structural problem: their CRMs are full of stale, incomplete company records. Lead data decays at roughly 30% per year. The standard response is manual research — SDRs spending hours filling in fields that should already be there — or expensive horizontal enrichment platforms that return generic, pre-scraped data that doesn't match the specific intelligence each firm actually needs.

ZoomInfo and Apollo offer broad coverage from static databases. Clay aggregates multiple vendors with LLM summaries, but uses generalised prompts to keep costs predictable — meaning every client gets the same surface-level output. Neither answers the question a RevOps director actually needs answered: does this company match our specific ICP, based on what they actually do?

The approach
Invisible by design
No new dashboard, no credits to manage, no behavior change required. LEAP executes inside the client's existing HubSpot workflow. The zero-click proof-of-value motion sends a prospect 30 of their own un-enriched leads, returned as a completed import sheet within two hours.
Vertical precision
Rather than matching a generic industry tag, LEAP parses unstructured text from company case studies and homepage copy to determine specific operational characteristics. The extraction schema is customised per client vertical — the capability Clay's cost model prevents them from offering.
Solo-operator economics
The financial model was built before the first line of code. At $0.003 variable COGS per lead and a $199/month base subscription, the unit economics support a one-person operation from day one.

Program management

LEAP was scoped as a formal program initiative before a line of code was written. A full project proposal established the competitive architecture, financial model, TAM/ICP definition, legal compliance framework, and quantitative validation methodology. A project charter followed, defining a 50-day critical path across four phases with an agent-augmented RACI matrix that delegates execution tasks to specialised LLM configurations while the founder retains strategic accountability.

Risk was identified and mitigated formally: scraper blocking routed through proxy nodes; GDPR/CASL exposure contained by restricting initial operations to North American domains; token budget bleed controlled by a programmatic pre-filter that bypasses LLM calls on low-signal pages.

Current status

Phase 1 in active development. Live FastAPI application with working /healthz and /enrich endpoints. Async webhook ingestion confirmed under 200ms. Target: 30-lead zero-click proof-of-value delivery at day 47.
